How to find us on Sunday

We meet on the second floor of an office tower. Here is the walk from the street to a seat.

  1. 9:45 AMArrive at the World Trade Center

  2. Before 10:00Take the elevator on the right to Floor 2

  3. 10:00 AMOur doors are straight ahead. The service starts.
